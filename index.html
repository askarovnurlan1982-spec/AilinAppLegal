What Ailin is

A self-guided app you use on your own terms. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to keep up with, and no score to maintain.

Cards you can read in a minute

Short, calm explanations of a single experience at a time — what it is, why it feels the way it does, and what usually helps.

SOS sessions for hard moments

Guided self-support audio for panic-like waves, intrusive thoughts and sleepless nights. Free SOS audio is available without a purchase.

Explanations, not reassurance

Five topic guides cover how anxiety works, the shape of a panic attack, body sensations, intrusive thoughts and the pillars of regulation.

Practices you can stop anytime

Three graded practices for living alongside a difficult thought, plus calming audio for sleep. Use them voluntarily, at your own pace.

A daily anchor, not a streak

One relevant card a day, chosen for you. Miss a day and nothing breaks — Ailin has no streaks, counters or notifications to chase.

Private by design

No account, no Ailin server, no ads and no tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Ailin is not medical care, and not an emergency service

Ailin is a self-guided educational and general-wellness app. It is not a physician, psychologist, psychotherapist or other licensed professional; not a medical device or diagnostic service; and not a substitute for professional evaluation, diagnosis, treatment or therapy. It does not diagnose any condition and cannot determine whether a symptom is caused by anxiety, a medical condition, medication or another cause.

Do not use Ailin to start, stop or change medication or professional treatment, and do not delay professional care because of information in the app. Seek prompt medical attention for new, severe, unusual or rapidly worsening symptoms.

The Ailin SOS feature is an in-app self-support exercise. It does not contact emergency services, assess danger, monitor you or alert another person. Our support and privacy email addresses are not monitored as an emergency channel. If you or another person may be in immediate danger, stop using the app and contact your local emergency service or a verified crisis resource in your country.

Ailin is intended for users aged 16 and older.

Subscription terms

Ailin PRO and Ailin PRO+ are auto-renewable subscriptions, offered with a monthly or annual billing period. Prices above are the planned United States prices; the price and billing period actually charged are the ones shown by Apple on the purchase screen at the time of purchase, and may vary by country, region, currency and taxes.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically for the selected period unless you cancel it through your Apple Account settings before the renewal date, in line with Apple’s rules. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Manage or cancel a subscription at support.apple.com/118428. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Eligible purchases can be restored in the app with Restore Purchases.
