3. Information Handled When You Use Ailin

3.1 Information stored locally on your device

The App may store limited information on your device so that it can remember your choices and function as expected. This may include:

App language;

onboarding completion;

settings and preferences;

local progress or completion state;

the current state of daily content;

audio playback or content-selection state; and

subscription entitlement or purchase-status information provided through Apple’s StoreKit framework.

This information is processed locally. Ailin does not receive or have remote access to it in the current version.

Local App information generally remains on your device until you remove it through available device or App controls or delete the App. Depending on your Apple settings, some App data may be included in a device or iCloud backup managed by Apple. You control those backups through your Apple settings.

3.2 Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or other paid feature, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Apple may process your Apple ID, payment and billing information, transaction records, device information, and purchase history under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

The App may receive limited transaction and entitlement information from Apple to provide or restore paid access. Ailin does not receive your full payment-card number and does not directly process App Store payments.

You can manage or cancel an App Store subscription through your Apple account settings. Deleting the App does not automatically cancel a subscription.

3.3 Information you choose to send to us

You do not need to provide your name, email address, health information, or other personal information to use the App’s core features.

If you contact us by email or through another support channel, we may receive:

your name and email address;

the contents of your message;

attachments you choose to send;

technical information you include, such as the App version, device model, or iOS version; and

records of our response and follow-up communications.

Please do not send medical records, diagnostic documents, government identification numbers, payment-card details, passwords, or information that is not necessary for us to handle your request.

Ailin is not a medical provider, emergency service, or crisis service. Support and privacy communications are not monitored as an emergency channel. Do not use them to request urgent medical or crisis assistance.

3.4 Health-related information

The App does not ask you to create a health profile, does not access HealthKit, and does not send your in-app activity to Ailin as health data.

You may voluntarily include health-related information in a support message. We do not require it. If you send it, we will use it only as reasonably necessary to understand and respond to your request, comply with applicable law, or protect legal rights. Where applicable law requires separate consent or authorization, we will request it before any additional use or delete the information when it is not needed.

We do not sell health-related information, use it for advertising, or use it to create advertising profiles.

3.5 Information the current App does not collect

The current App does not collect or transmit to Ailin:

precise or approximate location;

contacts, calendars, photos, camera data, or microphone recordings;

HealthKit data;

advertising identifiers;

browsing history;

biometric data;

user-created journal entries or chat messages;

diagnostic assessments;

analytics or behavioral profiles;

third-party advertising data; or

data for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it, de-identify it, or securely isolate it until deletion is reasonably possible, subject to legal and technical limitations.
